Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

Equipment, currency, upgrades and chapter progress are stored only on your device. Deleting the app or switching devices may lose your progress and currency, and the developer cannot restore it.

I bought Golden Spoons but didn't receive them.

Check your internet connection, then fully close and reopen the app. Completed transactions are credited on the next launch. If they still don't arrive, email us with the purchase date and item name.

How do I request a refund?

In-app purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are requested through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play right away without signing in.
