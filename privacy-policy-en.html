Kitchen Chaos Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

1. Information collected

Kitchen Chaos does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal information to developer-operated servers. The app contains no advertising, tracking or behavioral analytics SDKs.

2. Data stored on your device

Game progress, equipment, currency, upgrades and settings are stored locally on your device. The developer cannot access or restore this data. Deleting the app may delete this data; it is not synced across devices by the app.

3. In-app purchases

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store. The developer does not receive payment card details. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for information about Apple’s processing.

4. Children

The app does not collect personal information from players of any age.

5. Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
